# Transports

For World of Warcraft: Forever (Classic Plus).

Boats and zeppelins on the world map. Hover a dock and the crossing draws in, the tooltip names where that boat or zeppelin goes, and the dock at the other end glows. Click the pin to open that map. A dock with more than one destination offers a menu.

Every route is shown, Alliance and Horde. When a route belongs to one faction, the tooltip says so.

Nothing is drawn on the minimap. There is no departure timer and no walking path.
