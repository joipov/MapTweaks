# Map Tweaks

For World of Warcraft: Forever (Classic Plus).

Boat and zeppelin docks on the world map. Hover a dock and the crossing draws in, the tooltip names where that boat or zeppelin goes, and the dock at the other end glows. Click the pin to open that map. A dock with more than one destination offers a menu. Every route is shown, Alliance and Horde, and a route that belongs to one faction says so.

Unexplored parts of a zone are drawn in a dim blue tint, under the tiles you have already explored.

The zone under the cursor shows its level range after the name, colored like a quest level: red and orange are above you, yellow is your level, green and grey are below.

Dungeon and raid entrances use the retail icons. Point at one for its name, including each wing of a complex such as Blackrock Mountain. Click one to set a waypoint. They follow the world map's own "Show instance entrances" checkbox, and an entrance under a flight point or a dock stays hidden.

Each of the four can be turned off on its own, from Esc > Options > AddOns > Map Tweaks, from `/mt` or `/maptweaks`, or from the addon compartment.

Nothing is drawn on the minimap. There is no departure timer and no walking path.
